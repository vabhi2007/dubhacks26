# Alfred × SideShift — startup angle

> The "this is a company, not just a hack" narrative for the SideShift track: creator-video → affiliate distribution. One page; use it for the close and for judge Q&A.

## The wedge
Alfred already turns **an image into a buy decision** (Snap & Find → Worth-It → Smart Pay). SideShift points that same pipeline at the fastest-growing shopping surface: **creator video**. Every product shown in a review, haul, or short becomes a thing you can snap — and Alfred resolves it, prices it, and (with your mandate) buys it, while the creator who sent you there gets attributed.

## How it works
1. **Creator** posts a video (YouTube/TikTok/IG) as usual, or drops an Alfred link in the description.
2. **Viewer** sees something they want → snaps the frame in the extension, or opens the creator's Alfred link.
3. **Alfred** identifies the exact product (Perceptron + Claude resolution), finds the best price across sellers, and gives an honest Buy/Wait/Skip.
4. **Purchase** flows through the viewer's mandate and Smart Pay — the viewer buys at the best price, not just the first affiliate link.
5. **Attribution** flows back to the creator automatically. Alfred is the layer of trust *and* the attribution rail.

## Why it's better than today's affiliate links
- **Honest, not just promotional:** Alfred will say *wait, it drops next week* — which builds viewer trust in the creator, not churn.
- **Best price, any seller:** today's affiliate links lock you to one retailer; Alfred optimizes across sellers and card rewards, so the viewer wins too.
- **Works from a frame, not a link:** no creator setup required — any product shown on screen is snappable.
- **Cross-surface memory:** the snap in the extension, the follow-up "should I buy this?" in iMessage, and the receipt on the dashboard are one conversation.

## Who makes money, and how
- **Creators:** affiliate attribution on conversions, with transparent best-price buying that keeps audience trust.
- **Alfred:** a thin take on attributed GMV + optional premium (more mandates, voice approvals, household plans).
- **Viewers:** pay nothing; they save money (best price + optimal rewards) and avoid buyer's remorse.

## Why now
- Agentic commerce is arriving, but trust is the bottleneck — scoped mandates + human approval solve it.
- Creator video is where discovery already happens; the missing piece is a *trustworthy* buy-and-attribute layer.
- The hard parts already exist in Alfred: product resolution from pixels, honest verdicts, safe spending, cross-surface state.

## Defensibility
The moat is the **trust envelope** (mandates, approvals, one honest feed) plus **cross-surface data** on what a user actually buys and values — not the affiliate links, which are a commodity.

## Ask / next steps
Pilot with a handful of product-review creators; measure conversion lift and return rate vs. plain affiliate links. The thesis: *honesty converts better over time.*
